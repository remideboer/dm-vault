Varesha   
  
Varesha was Maris’ best friend and confidant, before Varesha was corrupted by the Tome.  
Varesha, pact met iets in de shaduw, vertelt niet aan Maris dat er gegeven wordt, maar daarna geind. Als het pact verbroken wordt, dan zal het zelf gaan innen. Details, of delen van het pact te vinden in dagboek in verborgen deel bibliotheek. Het boek wat op slot is, is onderdeel van het pact met de schaduw. Het voedt een langzame corruptie, Eledra kent alleen het verhaal wat Maris is verteld en wat Varesha het later gecorrumpeerd heeft, maar dat was het al vanaf het begin om via de schaduw voor haar als onderdeel meer kracht te krijgen. De paladijnen liepen tegen de muur, zij hadden te weinig kennis van het pact en magie om er daadwerkelijk wat aan te doen. Wel versloegen/verbanden’ Varesha naar de schaduwkant, maar Varesha sloeg met de kracht van het pact op de valreep terug. De paladijnen werden gedood en herrezen als bewakers van het pact. Ze maakte gebruik van wat de paladijnen kracht gaf, hun heilige vuur, en dat wordt hun onheilige anker als ondoden.  
  
Varesha bound herself in a pact with something that dwells within the Shadow. She never disclosed to Maris what was being surrendered — only what was to be gained. Should the pact ever be broken, the entity will come to collect its due itself.  
  
The particulars of the pact — or fragments thereof — are recorded in a journal concealed within a hidden alcove of the library of the small library of Varrow Keep.   
  
The locked tome in the underground chamber is not merely a book; it is a living piece of the pact, feeding a slow and creeping corruption. Holding it could corrupt. It is locked by a seal to protect the pact, but also by another seal to prevent further corruption. This was the paladins doing. In order to break the pact, the protective seal must be broken, then the second seal. Then the actual pact must be broken. The Tome was found in the remnants of **[Myth Drannor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Myth_Drannor)**.  
  
Eledra knows only the tale as Maris was told it — a version Varesha had already poisoned with half-truths. It was never honest. From the very beginning, Varesha used the pact as a conduit through the Shadow to draw greater power unto herself. Varesha found the book on a journey to uncover hidder magic artefacts.   
  
When the paladins uncovered what had been done, they were ill-equipped to unmake it. Their knowledge of shadow-binding and forbidden magic was too shallow. They managed to strike Varesha down and cast her into the Shadow — but at the last moment, she called upon the full strength of the pact and struck back. The paladins fell.  
  
They did not stay dead.  
  
Bound by the same pact they sought to destroy, they rose as its Wardens. Varesha turned the very thing that had made them holy against them — their sacred flame. That divine fire, which once consecrated their blades, now burns as an unholy anchor, chaining their souls to undeath and to the pact they could not break.
