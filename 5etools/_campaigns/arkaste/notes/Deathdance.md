The rules don’t specify unique ingredients for Deathdance. To craft one dose, use:  
* Poisoner’s kit and proficiency with it  
* Raw materials worth 100 GP—half of Deathdance’s 200 GP price  
* 40 days of downtime, assuming the standard crafting rate of 5 GP ofprogress per day  
The exact raw materials are up to the DM. A reasonable recipe might use a potent paralytic plant or venom, refined alchemical solvents, and stabilizing agents. The poisoner’s kit contains the basic equipment—vials, mortar and pestle, chemicals, and a glass stirring rod—but not the poisonous ingredients.
