# Ritual of Unbinding Blood  
ritual, abjuration  
Casting Time: 1 hour  
Range: Touch  
Components: V, S, M  
Duration: Instantaneous  
Classes: —  
The Ritual of Unbinding Blood is a rite used to destroy a magical bond between an object, creature and the creature or entity that has bound it. The ritual requires rare materials and must be performed at a specially prepared site.  
  
Material Components.   
The ritual requires:  
- dust obtained from a void creature slain in the vicinity of a void rift  
- holy water  
- a magic circle prepared beneath the object of binding  
- a radiant flame fueled by the dust of the void creature  
- a spell gem containing dispel evil and good  
- a portion of the essence of both the bound creature and the binder, incorporated into the spell gem  
  
The material components are consumed when the ritual succeeds.  
  
Ritual. The object of binding is placed within the magic circle and completely drenched in holy water. A radiant flame is then kindled using the void-creature dust as its fuel. While standing within the magic circle, the caster casts remove curse upon the object.  
  
Immediately afterward, the caster places the spell gem containing dispel evil and good into the radiant flame. The gem must have been prepared with the essence of both the bound creature and the binder. As the gem burns, its magic feeds the radiant flame, which begins to consume the magical binding upon the object.  
  
At the completion of the ritual, make a spellcasting ability check against the binding's Unbinding DC, determined by the DM based on the power and origin of the binding. On a success, the binding is destroyed and the object is freed from its magical bond. On a failure, the binding remains intact and the ritual components are consumed.  
  
Special condition — blood of the binding. The ritual cannot succeed unless the spell gem contains genuine essence from both sides of the bond: the creature that is bound and the creature or entity that created the binding. If either essence is absent or insufficient, the radiant flame burns without affecting the binding.  
  
At Higher Power. If the remove curse spell used as part of the ritual is cast using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the caster has advantage on the Unbinding check. If dispel evil and good is cast from a spell gem created using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the ritual automatically succeeds against bindings created by spells of 6th level or lower..
