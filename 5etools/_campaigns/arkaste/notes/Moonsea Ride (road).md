# Moonsea Ride (road)  
The Moonsea Ride is one of the most important trade  
and military routes in Faerûn, connecting the lands  
around the Moonsea to the kingdom of Cormyr, up to  
the Gnoll Peaks. The road winds through dangerous and  
wild regions, crossing valleys and mountains, making it  
a vital route for the transit of goods, troops, and adven-  
turers. Along its length, the Moonsea Ride is known for  
its variable maintenance, reflecting the stability of the  
regions it traverses.  
In Cormyr, in the area of the Thunder Peaks, the  
Moonsea Ride is carefully maintained by the royal army,  
as it serves as one of the main access points to the king-  
dom. This section of the road is of utmost importance,  
especially as it passes through the famous Tilver’s Gap, a  
strategic passage that connects the Moonsea and part of  
the Dalelands to the heart of Cormyr.  
The stretch of the Moonsea Ride that crosses Tilver’s  
Gap is constantly patrolled by the Purple Dragons and lo-  
cal guards, ensuring the safety of travelers and caravans  
passing through this region. However, the area is still  
considered dangerous, as bandits, monsters, and foreign  
spies often attempt to take advantage of the dense woods  
and steep slopes to ambush unsuspecting travelers.
