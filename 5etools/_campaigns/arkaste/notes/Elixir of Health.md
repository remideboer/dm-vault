## Elixir of Health  
When you drink this potion, you are cured of all magical contagions. In addition, the following conditions end on you: [Blinded](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#blinded_xphb), [Deafened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#deafened_xphb), [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb), and [Poisoned](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#poisoned_xphb).  
The clear, red liquid has tiny bubbles of light in it.  
  
### Components for Elixir of Health  

| Ingredient | Description |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Purified Unholy Water | Water that has been cleansed of negative energies. |
| Holy Water | Water that has been blessed, often used in rituals. |
| Powdered Water Opal | A gemstone ground into a fine powder, enhancing the potion's properties. |
  
Additional Notes  
* The elixir cures various ailments such as diseases, poisons, and conditions like blindness or deafness.  
* It does not heal any physical damage to the body, meaning it won't restore hit points or mend wounds.  
  
These components are essential for brewing the elixir, ensuring it retains its magical properties.
