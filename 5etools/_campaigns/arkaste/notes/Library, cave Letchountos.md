# Library, cave Letchountos  
In swampy area, ingang midden moeras, gekozen omdat er een hydra rondhangt, & of swamp goblins  
  
Mythn Drannor is waar Letchountos has made his library in forgotten ruins, he went there to study the necromantic magic that was used to kill his family and try to find counter measures. In this documentation he had to learn necromancy himself to learn how to counter it best, thats why Myth Drannor was a suitable place to study. Even though he documented his studies, he did not want the knowledge to fall into the wrong hands. That’s why his library, and de Nathlum especially, are heavily warded. The other reasing being to protect himself during his studies from the host of dangers that wander Myth Drannor..  
Letchountos left a memorandom of his motivations and reasoning, that’s in the book. Connect to it and you could talk to it as it were Letchountos himself. The book therefore has a form of sentience itself.
