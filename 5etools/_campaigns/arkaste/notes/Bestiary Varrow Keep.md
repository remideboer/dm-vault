## Arcamag Tiny Monstrosity (Shapechanger), Neutral  
Init. +0 | AC 13 (natural armor) | HP 22 | Speed 10, Fly 30 (hover) |  
STR -2 | DEX +0 | CON +2 | INT -3 | WIS +1 | CHA +4  
Skills: Stealth +4  
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11  
Languages: Understands Common but can't speak  
  
Camouflage: While attached to a host, advantage on Stealth checks.  
Shapechanger: Can polymorph into a small object (ring, wand, orb, rod, or scroll). Stats are the same in each form. Reverts to true form on death.  
False Appearance (Object Form Only): While motionless, indistinguishable from an ordinary object.  
  
Attach: Melee +2, 5 ft. Arcamag attaches to target that can cast spells. While attached, doesn't attack but causes the following changes to the host:  
  • 3/short rest: Host can cast a cantrip as a bonus action, but loses HP equal to its spellcaster level each time.  
  • Host gains one extra spell slot per spell level it can cast. Each time the extra slot is used, DC 10 CON save or gain one level of exhaustion.  
  • Each time host casts a spell, roll d20. On a 1, the spell fails but the slot is expended as the arcamag absorbs it.  
Can detach by spending 5 ft. of movement. Another creature can detach it with DC 15 STR check. Host can detach only after expending all spell slots. On detachment, host takes 1d4 Psychic per spellcaster level.  
Teleport (1/day): Teleports up to 60 ft. to an unoccupied space. Automatically detaches from host when used.  
CR 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
