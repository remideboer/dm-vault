# [BLACK UNICORN](https://www.5esrd.com/database/creature/unicorn-black)  
[lore](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Black_unicorn#cite_note-MF-1)  
![[Pasted image 20261027191827.png]]
Unicorn, Black Large Fey, Chaotic Evil  
Init. +3 | AC 13 | HP 67 | Speed 40 |  
STR +4 | DEX +3 | CON +2 | INT +1 | WIS +4 | CHA +3  
Skills: Perception +7, Stealth +6  
Imm: Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Paralyzed, Poisoned  
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17  
Languages: Sylvan, Abyssal, Elvish, Telepathy 60 ft.  
  
**Charge**: Moves min. 20 ft. straight toward target and hits with horn: +2d8 Piercing. DC 15 STR save or Prone.  
**Innate Spellcasting**: CHA (DC 15, +6). At will: detect evil and good, pass without trace, poison spray. 1/day: dispel evil and good, entangle, silence.  
**Magic Resistance**: Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.  
**Magic Weapons**: Weapon attacks are magical.  
  
**Multiattack**: One Hooves and one Horn attack.  
**Hooves**: Melee +7, 5 ft. 2d6+4 Bludgeoning.  
**Horn**: Melee +7, 5 ft. 1d8+4 Piercing.  
**Dark Touch (3/day)**: Touches creature with horn. 2d8+2 Necrotic.  
**Teleport (1/day)**: Teleports itself and up to 3 willing creatures within 5 ft. to a familiar location up to 1 mile away.  
  
**Legendary Actions (3/turn)**:  
**Hooves**: One Hooves attack.  
**Dark Shroud (2)**: Target within 60 ft. gains +2 AC until end of next turn.  
**Heal Self (3)**: Regains 2d8+2 HP.  
  
CR 5 (XP 1.800; PB +3)
