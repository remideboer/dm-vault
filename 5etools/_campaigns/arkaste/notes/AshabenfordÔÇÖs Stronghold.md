# Ashabenford’s Stronghold  
Locally known as “The Hold” or “Ashford’s Hold”  
  
Built generations ago in response to coordinated goblin raids from Beast Country and drow incursions out of Cormanthor, the Stronghold stands on the western bank of the River Ashaba, on a low natural rise overlooking the Moonsea Ride. Those threats have since quieted, but the walls remain — and the people within them have made it something more than a fortress. Over time it grew into the administrative and military heart of [Ashabenford](applenotes:note/89f819f4-ca61-4111-82bb-4a0f9b902c5f?ownerIdentifier=_b278a82f821370693820eb5509a1902d), housing the Council of Six’s overflow chambers, the Riders’ barracks, and a small but self-sufficient civilian community.  
  
The White Hart Inn sits just outside the outer gatehouse on the west bank — the first stop for most travelers before entering.  
  
Locations & NPCs  
  
1. Outer Gatehouse  
The first line of contact for visitors. Gate sergeant Brael Tinderholt (LG human veteran) has held this post for eleven years. He knows every resident by face and every smuggler’s trick by reputation. Quietly respected, rarely warm.  
  
2. Barn  
Run by Kaulvaeras Greymantle (LN moon half-elf), who operates a secondary stable here for the Riders’ mounts alongside his better-known stable in the village proper. His young assistant Pella is more comfortable with horses than people and is rumored to speak Elvish to the warhorses at night.  
  
3. Private Apartments  
Home to long-term residents, including Oswen Duskmantle (NG elderly human woman), a retired Rider who serves as the Hold’s unofficial memory. She knows where every body is buried — sometimes literally — and is a valuable source of local history for adventurers who take the time to sit with her.  
  
4. Smithy  
Darhlas (LN dwarf, Fighter/Expert) relocated his forge inside the walls after the last serious incursion. He makes and repairs weapons and armor for the Riders, and occasionally for civilians. Blunt in conversation, precise in craft. He gives discounts to those who have bled for the Hold.  
  
5. Provisioner’s Shop  
Owned by Mibbo Tangletoe (CN halfling, Neutral), whose shop looks like organized chaos but somehow always has what you need. He buys salvage with no questions asked — unless it’s obviously taken from locals, at which point he asks many questions, loudly.  
  
6. Trader’s Shop — Arms & Armor  
Iletian Blackeagle (LN human) originally worked as a coster agent for the Black Eagle trading company. He now sells weapons and armor from inside the Hold. He has connections in Hillsfar and can source unusual items on commission — for a premium and a wait.  
  
7. Bank  
Overseen by Maret Ashvale (LG human woman, Expert), appointed by the Council of Six to manage the Hold’s finances and tax collection from the surrounding farmsteads. Methodical, difficult to charm, impossible to bribe. She keeps two locked ledgers and trusts no one with both.  
  
8. Traveler’s Inn  
A spare but clean overflow inn for when the White Hart is full. Run by Berthas “Bert” Coalwick (NG human man), a broad ex-soldier turned cook. His stews are famous along the Moonsea Ride. He asks few questions and remembers every face.  
  
9. Tavern — “The Misthorn”  
The only place in the Hold where people genuinely laugh. Owned and run by Velsha (CG human woman, Neutral), a former bard from Suzail who drifted here years ago and never left. She plays every evening and knows more about everyone than she should. Adventurers who impress her get free drinks and useful rumors.  
  
10. Guild House / Council Chamber  
Used by the Council of Six when meeting inside the Hold is safer than gathering at Vinleaf’s residence in the village. The resident liaison is Grand Councilor Honorious Vinleaf (NG human) himself, who keeps a working office here. Polite, pragmatic, perpetually stretched thin.  
  
11. Temple  
Dedicated to Tyr with a side altar to Chauntea. The curate is Fazzir (LG human, Curate) — a capable but overworked man who handles ceremonies, heals injured Riders, and offers counsel. He assists the High Priest of Tyr’s temple in the village proper when needed.  
  
DM Secret: Fazzir’s predecessor, Ivlis, secretly led the Cult of Chaos. She departed on a supposed pilgrimage. A blood-red bell symbol has appeared twice on the Hold’s inner walls since her leaving. No one knows how it gets there.  
  
12. Inner Gatehouse  
The Hold’s last internal line of defense, always staffed by at least four Riders. Commanded by Lieutenant Asha Brightspear (LG human woman, Knight) — young, sharp, and intensely loyal to Vinleaf. She has never lost a gate and intends to keep it that way.  
  
13. Inner Bailey — Riders’ Barracks  
The training ground, armory, and barracks for the Riders of Mistledale garrisoned here. Roughly sixteen active Riders are stationed at any given time. Training master is Halvan Greyboot (LN human, Veteran) — scarred, sparse with words, observes everything. He has trained Riders for twenty years and quietly believes the current generation is the best he’s seen.  
  
14. Fortress — Seat of the Grand Councilor  
The administrative core of the Hold. Vinleaf’s private quarters and working chambers are here, alongside an office occupied by Almaestaddamir Auldcastle (Small, Chaotic Good Mage) — the gnome alchemist who has lived in Ashabenford most of his life and currently advises the Councilor on arcane threats. He once proposed catapulting kegs of smokepowder at raiders. Vinleaf declined, politely, twice.  
  
Tone & Use  
  
The Hold is not unwelcoming — but it reads people quickly. It is a community that has lived with the knowledge that the wilderness presses close, and has built something stable in spite of that. Adventurers who prove useful find doors opening; those who cause trouble find them closing just as fast. The Council of Six values pragmatism above ceremony, and so does everyone who works within these walls.
