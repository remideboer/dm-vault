## Daughter of Urgathoa Large Undead, Neutral Evil  
![Afbeelding](Attachments/9A41C7EC-2CE8-452A-83D4-5574204FE6E6.png)  
**Init. +3** | **AC 17** | HP 115 | Hits 12 | Speed Fly 40 (hover) |  
STR +8 | DEX +3 | CON — | INT +4 | WIS +3 | CHA +5  
Save: WIS +7  
Skills: Acrobatics +7, Bluff +9, Fly +11, Intimidate +9, Perception +7, Religion +11, Sense Motive +7, Spellcraft +7, Stealth +0  
Imm: Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned (Undead Immunities)  
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17  
Languages: Abyssal, Common, Infernal, Necril  
  
**Desecrate Aura** (20 ft.): Undead within aura gain +1 to attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws. Turned undead within aura get a new save each round. Clerics attempting to turn undead within aura do so at disadvantage.  
Channel Resistance: +4 bonus to saves against effects that turn or destroy undead.  
**Great Claw**: Treated as evil for bypassing resistance; scores a critical hit on a 19–20 for triple damage.  
  
**Multiattack**: One Great Claw and one Claw attack.  
***Great Claw***: Melee +9, 10 ft. 2d6+8 Slashing. On hit, target makes ***DC 17 CON*** save or contracts Bubonic Plague.  
***Claw***: Melee +9, 10 ft. 1d8+8 Slashing.  
  
**Spellcasting**: ***WIS (DC 14*** base, higher spells ***DC 14 + spell level − 1***). Cleric spells (6th-level):  
  **Cantrips**: bleed, detect magic, guidance, resistance.  
  1st (4 slots): cause fear, command, divine favor, obscuring mist, shield of faith.  
  2nd (3 slots): death knell, hold person, inflict wounds (2d10), resist energy, spiritual weapon.  
  3rd (2 slots): bestow curse, contagion, dispel magic, inflict wounds (5d10).  
  
**Bubonic Plague** (Disease): Injury via Great Claw. ***DC 17 CON*** save at end of each long rest. Fail: 1d4 CON damage and Poisoned (Fatigued) until next save. Two consecutive saves to recover.  
  
CR 8 (XP 3.900; PB +3)  
  
**Cause Fea**r: One creature within 60 ft., DC 14 WIS save or Frightened 1 minute. 1st level.  
**Command**: One creature within 60 ft., DC 14 WIS save or follows one-word command. 1 round.  
**Divine Favor**: +1d4 to attack and damage rolls. Concentration, 1 minute.  
**Obscuring Mist**: 20-ft. radius heavily obscured. Concentration, 1 hour.  
**Shield of Faith**: +2 AC. Concentration, 10 minutes.  
**Death Knell**: Creature at 0 HP dies; caster gains 1d8 HP and +2 STR for 10 minutes. DC 15 CON save negates.  
**Hold Person**: One Humanoid within 60 ft., DC 15 WIS save or Paralyzed. Concentration, 1 minute.  
**Inflict Wounds** (2nd): Melee spell attack, 3d10 Necrotic.  
**Resist Energy**: Resistance to one damage type. 1 hour.  
**Spiritual Weapon**: Bonus action summons weapon; bonus action attack +5, 1d8+3 Force. Concentration, 1 minute.  
**Bestow Curse**: Touch, DC 16 WIS save or cursed (disadvantage on one ability check/save, or -1d4 to attacks). Concentration, 1 minute.  
**Contagion**: Touch, DC 16 CON save or diseased. No concentration, 7 days.  
**Dispel Magic**: End one spell of 3rd level or lower; higher levels require DC 10 + spell level check.  
**Inflict Wounds** (3rd): Melee spell attack, 5d10 Necrotic.  
  
## Child of Urgathoa Large Undead, Neutral Evil  
Init. +3 | AC 17 | HP 120 | Hits 12 | Speed Fly 40 |  
STR +6 | DEX +3 | CON +4 | INT +2 | WIS +6 | CHA +5  
Vuln: Radiant 10  
Imm: Disease, Paralyzed, Poison, Unconscious; Death effects  
  
Share Blessings: When destroyed, explodes in a 10-ft. burst of infectious rot. All creatures in range exposed to Pallid Plague.  
  
Multiattack: One Scything Blade and one Pallid Touch attack.  
Scything Blade: Melee +7, 10 ft. 2d10+6 Slashing + 1d6 Necrotic. On critical hit: +1d10 Slashing. Can trip on hit.  
Pallid Touch: Melee +7, 10 ft. 2d10+6 Necrotic. On hit, target exposed to Pallid Plague.  
Spellcasting: WIS (DC 16). 2/day each: harm, sudden blight. 2/day: bind undead.  
Reap: Scything Blade attack compared against AC of up to two creatures within reach. Roll damage once, apply to each target hit.  
Wake the Dead (1/minute): Touch a creature that died within the last minute; DC 16 WIS save or it rises as a Zombie Lord under the child's control. Max 4 Zombie Lords controlled at once.  
  
Pallid Plague (Disease, Virulent): DC 16 CON save at end of each long rest.  
  Stage 1: Carrier, no symptoms (3 days).  
  Stage 2: 3d8 Necrotic, Doomed 1 (1 day).  
  Stage 3: 3d8 Necrotic, Doomed 1, Drained 1 (1 day).  
  Stage 4: 4d8 Necrotic, Doomed 2, Drained 1 (1 day).  
  Stage 5: 4d8 Necrotic, Doomed 3, Drained 2 (1 day).  
  Stage 6: Death. Rises as a Zombie Lord.  
  
CR 8 (XP 3.900; PB +3)  
  
Harm (4th): Touch or 30 ft., one living creature or willing undead. Living: 5d8 Necrotic, DC 16 CON save for half. Undead: restore 5d8 HP. 3-action version: 30-ft. burst, all living and undead.  
Sudden Blight (4th): 120 ft., 20-ft. burst. All living creatures DC 16 CON save or 5d10 Necrotic; half on success. Diseased creatures at disadvantage on save. Destroys Small/Tiny non-magical plants in area.  
Bind Undead (3rd): 30 ft., one mindless undead of CR 3 or lower. DC 16 WIS save or controlled for 1 day (minion). Ends if attacked by caster or ally.
