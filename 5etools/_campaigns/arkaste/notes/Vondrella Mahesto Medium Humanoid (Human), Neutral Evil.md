  4th (3 slots): cure wounds (4d8+8) ×2, spell immunity, unholy blight.  
  
Lootable: +2 scale mail, +1 scythe, masterwork light crossbow with 10 bolts, cloak of resistance +1, ring of protection +2, pearl of power (2nd level), 2× potion of cure moderate wounds, potion of remove disease, 40 pp.  
  
CR 7 (XP 3.200; PB +3)

Spiritual Weapon: Bonus action summons weapon; bonus action attack +7, 1d8+4 Force. Concentration, 1 minute.  
Bestow Curse: Touch, DC 17 WIS save or cursed (disadvantage on one ability/save, or −1d4 to attacks). Concentration, 1 minute.  
Blindness/Deafness: One creature within 30 ft., DC 17 CON save or Blinded or Deafened (no concentration). 1 minute.  
Contagion: Touch, DC 17 CON save or diseased with chosen disease. 7 days.
