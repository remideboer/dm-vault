**THE ETTIN QUEST**  
A local farmer outside a town has been losing livestock and a section of his fence line has been demolished three nights running. Something large is sleeping in the tree line by day and wandering by night. He thinks it's a troll. It isn't.  
It's an Ettin — two-headed, roughly four metres tall, confused and increasingly agitated. Acting on instinct rather than intent. It has wandered out of the northern forest margins, displaced by something further in that it won't go back near. The two heads disagree constantly on what to do next, which is why it keeps circling the same farmland rather than moving on — one head wants to go north, the other refuses.  
  
The farmers want it gone. The town elder will pay for containment rather than killing — it hasn't harmed anyone yet and she's practical enough to know dead Ettins attract attention from whatever drove it out of the forest.  
  
The cultist of chaos drove it out of it's cavern.   
  
The resolution options are classic and open: drive it off, contain it, communicate with it if anyone speaks Giant or finds another way, or kill it. The two heads respond differently to different players — one is aggressive, one is frightened. A clever group can use that split against itself or exploit it to de-escalate. A less clever group will probably just hit the aggressive head until the frightened one agrees to leave.  
  
Either way the players learn that things are being displaced from the mountains, hills. Something is pushing them south. It has been doing this for about three weeks — which is roughly when the Heartwood's outer margins started failing.  
  
If they find a way to restore the Ettin's cavern, then they will have a new ally.
