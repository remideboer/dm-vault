# Rules & Intent  
It should be noted that, for all intents and purposes, these adventures can be lethal. Encounters are not balanced, it’ up to the players to figure out how to deal with potential hostiles.  Unless the players are cautious, they will have difficulty reaching level 3 alive. **Ghezelon** (priest in ?) and Thalara Moonleaf** **(druid, somewhere,) are both able to bring someone back from the dead, but with difficulty, and it will require a great favor to convince them to resurrect a stranger.
