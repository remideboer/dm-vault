## Person and story

**Backstory.** Mel Anom Ellon was not always his name. He was a High Elf who had found everything a Elf could want — a wife he loved, and a child on the way. Fifty-eight years they had built a life together. The pregnancy was rare and precious, as all elven children are. They were travelling when it happened. An orc raid on a human settlement. Wrong road, wrong moment. Not targeted. Not personal. Just chaos, and they were in the middle of it. He fought. It wasn't enough. When it was over he hunted the warband. Tracked them across whatever terrain they fled into. Found every last one. Killed them all. Then stood in the silence after and felt nothing close to better. He discarded his birth name after that. It belonged to someone else — someone who had a future. He renamed himself Mel Anom Ellon. The dark future. What remained when everything ahead of you is gone. That was over a hundred years ago. Since then he drifts. Takes work. Moves slowly and deliberately, as an Elf does when he has nowhere to be. He holds no allegiances, no hatreds, no preferences. Orcs, humans, elves — all equal in his indifference. He does not fear death. He simply has not met it yet.
