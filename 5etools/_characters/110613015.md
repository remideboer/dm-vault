**Backstory.** Faelar Daeran, a male wood elf, was born in the heart of the wilds, far from the bustling towns and cities. He now resides in a region near the town of Phandalin, where he carries out his druidic duties with unwavering dedication. In his youth, Faelar Daeran was a spirited and adventurous young wood elf, much like his peers in the heart of the forest. Raised in the time-honored traditions of the wood elven culture of, Faelar embraced the harmonious bond between his people and the natural world. He was a normal boy, curious and nearly fearless, often venturing into the dense woods with his friends to explore the secrets that lay within. They roamed the forests, their bows and arrows a natural extension of their bodies. The wild beauty of the forest enchanted him, and he reveled in the art of archery and the thrill of the hunt, skills he honed through the guidance of his parents and his family.

Faelar's upbringing emphasized the importance of respecting the ancient wood elven ways. His parents instilled in him a deep reverence for the balance of nature, teaching him to take only what was needed from the forest and to give back in return. He shared a strong and loving bond with his family, who were also skilled hunters and guardians of the forest. The lessons of his childhood revolved around preserving the delicate equilibrium of the natural world, and Faelar learned to appreciate the wisdom of the woods, the creatures that inhabited it, and the magic that flowed through its veins. As he grew, he exhibited a great respect for the traditions and values that shaped his wood elven heritage, fostering a sense of connection with the land and its inhabitants that would ultimately guide his path as a protector of nature.

As a youngling, he was part of a small lively elven hunting party when Faelar's life took an unexpected turn, leaving him forever changed. The tranquil forest was shattered by the sudden appearance of a marauding band of Orogs, creatures who held no regard for the delicate balance of the natural world. Faelar's hunting party was caught off guard. Faelar was knocked unconscious right at the start of the fierce skirmish. He just managed to get a glimpse of his best friend being killed from behind by the swing of an Orog Axe. As the battle subsided, Faelar began to stir, and just as he realized he was the only survivor, the Orogs, seeing him awaken, decided it would be a cruel amusement to present him as a living trophy to their chieftain.

For days, Faelar endured captivity in his confined, cage-bound position, bearing witness to the Orogs' relentless disregard for the natural world. His life was in peril as the chieftain planned to take his head as a grim trophy of their victory over the elves. However, in the midst of his darkest hour, hope arrived in the form of assault by a druidic circle known as the Circle of Moon. Alerted by disturbances to nature by the Orog's raiding band, this Circle of Moon launched a courageous assault on the camp. The Circle liberated Faelar from his shackles and cage just in the nick of time. 

Saved by the wise and compassionate female Shield Dwarf Thraina Stoneforge who personally tended to his wounds and set him free, Faelar was deeply moved by her kindness and the Circle's unwavering commitment to nature's preservation and harmony between the races.

Right there Faelar made a life-altering decision. Driven by the memory of his harrowing imprisonment, the rescue by the druids, and his newfound appreciation for the power of druidic magic, he chose to embrace the path of the druid. Faelar had witnessed the unity of various races, with the good-hearted druids, in the name of preserving the balance of nature, and he vowed to become a guardian of the natural world and to learn more about the other races. This was really the first time he met other races from upclose and them united striving for a common goal, this made a big impact on the young Faeral. 
Eventhough the perilious nature of the encounter, it made him open mind and heart to the other races. 

Since his druidic training, Faelar has become a wanderer, exploring the wide and diverse lands of Toril. Faelar's encounter with the Orogs and the druid party changed him profoundly, leaving him with a deep appreciation for other cultures and races, especially dwarves and gnomes. Yet, his apprehension towards humans and other creatures with a limited lifespan lingers, as he believes their short lifespans often lead to a lack of consideration for the long-term consequences of their actions, particularly when it comes to the natural world.

As Faelar embarked on his druidic journey, he dedicated himself to understanding the intricate connection between nature and magic. Later in his travels, while fervently protecting nature, Faelar came into conflict with a Dragonborn sorcerer, Nyirrhed Zranaar. Their confrontation reached a climactic moment when the sorcerer unleashed a powerful spell of arcane magic. Faelar, instinctively invoking a counter-spell rooted in the magic of nature, succeeded in mitigating much of the impact. However, the fusion of the two powerful energies created a lasting change in his right eye. His once-green eye transformed into a brilliant shade of purple, signifying the fusion of nature and arcane magic. This change rendered Faelar more sensitive to the residual imprints from arcane magical disturbances in nature. The purple eye served as a constant reminder of his commitment to both protect and understand the intricate balance of the world, where nature and magic intersected.

Faelar Daeran's unyielding commitment to preserving the delicate balance of nature drive him to protect the realms he wanders through, ensuring that the lessons of the natural world are not forgotten.

As Faelar began his journey as a druid under the mentorship of the Circle of Moon, he found himself drawn to the crossbow as an alternative to the traditional longbow commonly favored by his wood elven kin. The influence of the Circle, with its members from various races, including gnomes and dwarves known for their craftsmanship, introduced Faelar to the art of crafting and using crossbows. The dwarves and gnomes' expertise in engineering and precision combined with Faelar's innate connection to nature sparked his curiosity, ultimately leading to a preference for the crossbow. 

Faelar discovered that the crossbow provided a unique blend of accuracy and power, allowing him to strike with precision while conserving energy for his druidic spells and nature-infused abilities. The crossbow's mechanical nature appealed to his fascination with the intricate workings of the world and the balance between nature and technology. It was a symbol of the unity he sought to achieve between the teachings of the Circle and his wood elven heritage, a tool that allowed him to protect the natural world with efficiency and skill. As a result, Faelar grew to favor the crossbow, utilizing it as an extension of his newfound druidic powers and his deep respect for the interconnectedness of all things in the realms.

**Allies.** Thraina Stoneforge

**Organizations.** Circle the Moon

**Enemies.** Nyirrhed Zranaar

**Other notes.** Faelar is not much of a talker, he is observant and contemplative. Can sometimes appear stern, but that’s usually because he has spent long times alone in nature.

Struck by Eldath and the Path of Life.
While lying unconscious, struck down in battle fighting along side Hjoldor and Zyone, amidst the chaos of clashing swords and the cries of the fallen, Faelar Daeran experienced a profound spiritual awakening. The soft, serene voice of Eldath, the Goddess of Peace, Singing Waters, and Guardian of Groves, whispered into his soul. Her words, gentle yet imbued with profound power, reached him in the depths of his unconsciousness: “Extend your path of life and cross over to my domain. You have tended and cared for nature; now, it’s time to start shielding the innocent and the righteous. Remember your past. I will grant you my blessings if you do.”

Upon awakening, Faelar’s gaze fell upon the creatures trapped and suffering around him, mirroring the peril he once faced and overcame with the aid of the druids. This moment of reflection, combined with the divine encounter, catalyzed a deep, inner transformation. Resolved to heed Eldath’s call, Faelar chose to expand his vocation, incorporating the principles of peace and protection into his life’s mission. Drawing upon his druidic foundation, he began to meditate on Eldath’s teachings, seeking to intertwine her divine will with his own dedication to the natural world. He carved the holy symbol of Eldath in his druidic staff as to symbolize the symbiosis of his druidic and life path ways.
