## Person and story

**Backstory.** Alora was born as Adeline Victoria Goldleaf, to a wealthy family. she grew up loved by her parents, but that love was overwhelmed by how strict they were. She was raised to be a prim and proper lady, something that never quite stuck. She was also told to abandon any dreams about her magic, and instead to focus on the life her parents build. Before Alora was born her parents found a gold mine. they made a fast growing business out of it. Her father took charge, and used whatever and whoever he had at his disposal. He was cruel to the people around him, except for his family. Alora noticed and tried multiple times to stand up for the workers and change her fathers mind, to no avail. Being unhappy in her home and knowing her family expected her to take on this cruel legacy one day, she decided to leave when she was 22 (8 years ago). She took one of the horses and never looked back, promising herself she would be better. The years since then have been spent under a false name, trying to stay out of the spotlight. it's only recently that she decided she could do more.
