## Person and story

**Backstory.** Aye, mining away deep in the mountain, finding silver, gold and shiny crystals might seem like the life for any old dwarf, but this dwarf wasn't old! Seeking out the farmlands in the glens might seem like the life for any stuffy dwarf, but this dwarf wasn't stuffy! Climbing the highest mountains and touching their cairns might seem like the life for any adventurous dwarf, but this dwarf was no ranger! No, Brùnach was always fascinated by the tunes the miners hummed, the rhythmic chopping of the hand plough by the farmers on the land and the wheezing of the old dwarven rangers as their tired old bodies ascended a mountain. When, as a young lad, he heard the most majestic instrument of them all - the bagpipes, played at his uncle's funeral - he was sold! His fellow dwarves in the mines, on the fields or mountain tops did not understand his desire to make music his profession but he cared very little. As soon as he was old enough he went out on his own carrying few provisions, his bagpipes, flute and his fiddle and went on to see the world. Of course any dwarf was taught to defend themselves, their clan and, most importantly, their possessions they worked so hard for. His fighting skills turned out to be invaluable as, when he moved out into the world, he often found it unfair towards the common man and favored towards the rich and unlawful. He quickly learned that if he wanted any justice it was best to dish it out himself and never bother with the lawmen afterwards. After many years on the road, without a house or a certain place to sleep this night but his belly full, Brùnach heads towards his next adventure, cheerfully humming a familair old tune...
