## Person and story

**Backstory.** Born in a quiet village of Schwarzdor where superstition grew faster than wheat, I learned early that fear can kill as surely as blade or flame. The air there was always heavy with whispers. Curses blamed for spoiled harvests, disappearances spoken of only after dark, and prayers offered more out of habit than hope. When a coven was uncovered in the nearby woods, the village did not find salvation. It found chaos. What followed was fire, accusation, and ruin. In the aftermath, someone I cared for was lost to it—whether as victim, or condemned as witch, I was never able to discover. That uncertainty did not fade with time. It hardened. It became purpose. I was taken in by an austere order sworn to excise evil wherever it took root. There I was remade: taught to read the tremor of a lie in a man’s voice, to endure charms without yielding, and to strike without hesitation when corruption revealed itself. But steel and scripture were only the surface of their doctrine. Beneath it lay something heavier: vigilance without rest, skepticism without comfort, and the terrible burden of judgment carried in silence. Now I walk from settlement to settlement along the borderlands where law thins and darkness gathers. I hunt what others fear to name. Some call me protector. Others whisper of an inquisitor who brings worse judgment than the crimes he seeks to punish. I no longer pretend to know which is true. I only know the line must be held.
